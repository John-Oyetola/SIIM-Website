Estate Operational Prioritization Tiers

Multi-site fleet status categorizes venues dynamically into three operational condition states.

NORMAL TIER GREEN

Within Baseline

Operating stably within configured thresholds. Telemetry metrics confirm nominal pH range, normal grease deposition trajectory, and healthy hydraulic flow speeds across all installed sensors.

REVIEW TIER AMBER

Trend Review

Anomaly detected or accumulation rate approaching warning limits. System flags locations where grease build-up is accelerating or flow speed is decaying before critical thresholds are breached.

ACTION REQUIRED RED

Action Required

Active operational threshold breach requiring immediate technical dispatch. Triggers prioritized engineering escalation for localized extract hot spots (500µm+) or severe trade effluent chemical spikes.

4-Tier Role-Based Access Hierarchy

National & Estate Managers

TIER 1 • PORTFOLIO

Regional Managers

TIER 2 • REGIONAL CLUSTER
