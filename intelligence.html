Editorial Process Framework

The Proactive Governance Loop

How continuous telemetry transforms reactive crisis management into structured prevention.

01

MONITOR

CONTINUOUS PHYSICAL TELEMETRY

Continuous edge monitoring of extract duct loading, thermal gradients, and effluent discharge. High-frequency sampling sensors gather continuous baseline measurements across ventilation and drainage infrastructure.

02

IDENTIFY

PATTERN & DRIFT ALGORITHMS

Automated trend modeling spots baseline drift, localized accumulation, and chemical anomalies early. Algorithms differentiate normal operational peaks (e.g., scheduled pot wash) from developing system faults.

03

ALERT

CONTEXT-RICH NOTIFICATION ROUTING

Intelligent routing delivers context-rich alerts to authorized managers without notification fatigue. Notifications specify exact location, metric deviation, active duration, and recommended corrective action.

04

INTERVENE

TARGETED PREVENTATIVE ACTION

Targeted maintenance, localized degreasing, or chemical rebalancing before operational failure occurs. Interventions are executed proactively during routine windows, eliminating emergency closures.

05

EVIDENCE

AUDIT & COMPLIANCE LOGGING

Time-stamped historical logs generate indisputable compliance records for insurers, water authorities, and environmental health officers, verifying continuous adherence to BESA TR/19 and trade effluent standards.

Risk Assessment & Comparative Analysis

Comprehensive Protection Across Both Vectors

Comparing the operational and compliance hazards eliminated by SIIM across ventilation and drainage networks.

VECTOR 01

Extract & Ventilation Hazards

  • •
    Ductwork Fire Hazards Uncontrolled grease loading in unmonitored canopy risers creates severe fuel sources for commercial kitchen fires.
  • •
    Catastrophic Business Interruption Unscheduled extract outages during peak dining service force immediate kitchen closures and lost revenue.
  • •
    Insurance Non-Compliance Penalties Failure to maintain verified TR/19 cleaning records can invalidate property fire insurance policies.
  • •
    Premature Fan Motor Degradation Restricted airflow forces extract fans to work against elevated static pressure, burning out motors prematurely.
VECTOR 02

Wastewater & Drainage Hazards

  • •
    Foul Water Back-Up & Flooding Grease accumulation and fatbergs choke drainage lines, resulting in destructive scullery water back-ups.
  • •
    Emergency Store Closures Sanitation laws require immediate store shutdown upon drainage failure, causing severe financial damage.
  • •
    Trade Effluent Consent Penalties Discharging non-compliant acidic or grease-heavy trade effluent triggers fines from regional water authorities.
  • •
    Customer Complaints & Odour Issues Stagnant grease and chemical off-gassing escape through floor drains, creating foul dining room odours.
