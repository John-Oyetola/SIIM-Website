SIIM Telemetry Services

Continuous Visibility Across Critical Systems

A single manual inspection provides only an isolated snapshot. SIIM monitors what leaves your kitchen and drainage infrastructure in real time, turning continuous physical measurements into actionable operational intelligence.

System 01 • Kitchen Ventilation Intelligence

Extract Ductwork & Grease Accumulation

Move beyond periodic manual scrape inspections. SIIM tracks grease deposition rates, thermal profiles, and airflow characteristics to identify localized build-up and guide cleaning intervals.

Micron Level Tracking

0 - 500+ µm

Continuous measurement across extract duct runs, distinguishing overall duct average accumulation from localized hot spots approaching the critical 500 micron TR/19 threshold.

TR/19 Cleaning Projection

DYNAMIC ACCUMULATION

Dynamic calculation of 'Days to Next Clean' based on active daily grease accumulation trends rather than arbitrary calendar schedules, preventing premature maintenance while eliminating insurance liability.

Airflow Velocity & Fan Performance

VELOCITY (m/s)

Monitors extract air movement to verify fan performance, detect duct restriction, and identify ventilation drop-offs before kitchen heat or smoke builds up on line.

Thermal Profile & Flash Risk

TEMPERATURE (°C)

Correlates temperature with grease vaporization rates and peak cooking schedules, alerting operators to abnormal thermal spikes that elevate fire risk in high-heat canopy risers.

System 02 • Drainage & Trade Effluent Intelligence

Outgoing Wastewater & Trade Effluent

Restaurants discharge a fluctuating mix of fats, food residues, detergents, and sanitizing chemicals. SIIM monitors conditions as water leaves the building, establishing baseline patterns and recording abnormal discharge events.

EFFLUENT TELEMETRY FLOW PIPELINE
STAGE 01

Incoming Supply

Clean mains water inlet into commercial kitchen & prep stations.

STAGE 02

Kitchen & Scullery

Dishwashing, pot wash, grease traps, and heavy cleaning operations.

STAGE 03

SIIM Effluent Telemetry

Continuous pH, flow rate, temperature, and line capacity measurement.

pH Condition & Chemical Surges

pH 0 - 14 SCALE

Detects sharp acidic or alkaline surges (e.g., concentrated caustic cleaning agent discharges) that risk internal pipe corrosion, biological grease trap breakdown, or trade effluent compliance violations.

Wastewater Temperature Context

THERMAL (°C)

Identifies sustained hot water surges and washdown cycles, providing thermal context to diagnose chemical emulsification and grease solidifying downstream in cooler drainage infrastructure.

Flow Velocity & Volumetric Rate

VELOCITY (m/s) • FLOW (L/s)

Measures discharge speed and volumetric throughput across monitored pipe runs, establishing site baseline hydraulic loading during peak operating hours versus non-service windows.

Pipe Capacity & Restriction Risk

FILL % • BACK-UP ALARM

Evaluates liquid depth and velocity decay patterns to flag developing fatbergs, line chokes, and back-up risks well before physical kitchen flooding or operational stoppage occurs.

Regulatory & Compliance Positioning

Measured Intelligence, Not Assumptions

SIIM does not claim that pH or grease measurements alone replace comprehensive trade effluent sampling. Instead, continuous monitoring provides an indisputable, time-stamped operational history that helps teams investigate anomalies early, reduce compliance exposure, and avoid emergency callouts.
