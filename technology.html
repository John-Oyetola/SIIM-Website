Sensor Array Architecture

Engineered hardware components designed to deliver sub-millimeter diagnostic precision.

📡

SIIM Acoustic Impedance Sensors

Non-invasive acoustic wave sensors mounted directly onto exhaust ducting and drainage pipelines. Transmits ultrasonic pulses through metal walls to measure internal grease & scale deposit thickness without drilling or dismantling.

Precision: ±0.1mm deposit resolution
🌀

Airflow Velocity & Thermal Probes

Multi-point thermodynamic probes measuring extraction duct airflow velocity (0–30 m/s), differential static pressure, and exhaust temperatures up to 450°C to detect fan motor degradation and flashover risks.

Operating Temp: Up to 450°C continuous rating
⚡

Real-Time Cellular Telemetry Mesh

Low-power LTE-M / NB-IoT cellular mesh gateway connecting all onsite sensors to the SIIM cloud telemetry engine. Features offline buffer memory caching to prevent data loss during power outages.

Latency: Sub-60s encrypted telemetry feeds
